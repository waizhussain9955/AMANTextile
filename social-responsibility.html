Our Commitments

Ethical Principles in Practice

Worker Welfare & Safety

Strict enforcement of fair wages, safe ergonomic working conditions, medical insurance, healthcare checkups, and zero tolerance for child or forced labor.

Eco-Dyeing & Water Treatment

Equipped with Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP) to purify wastewater before disposal, conserving clean water resources and reducing chemical impact.

Clean Energy & Waste Reduction

Progressive integration of rooftop solar energy, energy-efficient LED machinery, and recycling 100% of fabric cutting scraps.
