About Our Company

We are a leading textile manufacturing facility specializing home textile and babies Items and Kitchen Items. With a legacy spanning over 16 years, our factory stands as a beacon of unwavering commitment to quality craftsmanship and socially responsible practices.

As a trusted supplier, Our Products exported to some of the largest international brands and department stores across North America and Europe.

OUR MISSION

Our humble beginnings make us proud. Having evolved from a small fabric exporter to a composite textile processing unit has been a challenging and rewarding journey spanning Second generations.
