About Our Company

We are a leading textile manufacturing facility specializing in crafting premium knitted and woven apparel, including baby wear, as well as high-quality home textiles. With a legacy spanning over 30 years, our factory stands as a beacon of unwavering commitment to quality craftsmanship and socially responsible practices.

As a trusted supplier, our garments are exported to some of the largest international brands and department stores across North America and Europe.
